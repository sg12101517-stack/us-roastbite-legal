Privacy Policy

App: RoastBite - Funny Food Logger (Bundle ID: us.roastbite.foodroast) · Last Updated: November 23, 2026

1. Zero Data Collection & 100% Offline Architecture

RoastBite is engineered from the ground up as a strictly offline, zero-account food journal and humor engine. Unlike cloud AI apps, RoastBite processes every meal entry, keyword parse, and persona roast completely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, rent, sell, or analyze any personal data. Specifically, RoastBite does not collect or access:

  • Names, email addresses, account logins, passwords, or phone numbers
  • Your meal entries, snack logs, roast cards, Fuel Scores, or daily streaks
  • Camera, microphone, location, contacts, health store, or photo library permissions
  • Cloud AI APIs, third-party analytics SDKs, crash trackers, or advertising identifiers (IDFA)

2. On-Device Storage & Optional Exports

All meal logs, persona preferences, and daily statistics remain inside your iPhone or iPad's local app sandbox. Deleting the app removes its local data. When you tap "Share Roast Card" or "Export Local Backup (JSON)", the resulting PNG or JSON file is shared strictly through the iOS Share Sheet to the destination you choose.

3. In-App Purchases (Apple StoreKit 2)

Optional RoastBite Pro plans (Lifetime, Annual, and Monthly) and "Restore Purchases" requests are processed exclusively by Apple Inc. through the App Store and StoreKit 2. The developer never receives or views your payment card details, billing address, or Apple ID credentials.

4. Children's Privacy

RoastBite is rated 4+ and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13, because zero personal data ever leaves your device.
