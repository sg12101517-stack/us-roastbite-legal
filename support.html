RoastBite Support

Thank you for using RoastBite - Funny Food Logger (Bundle ID: us.roastbite.foodroast). Below are answers to common questions and our direct support contact.

Frequently Asked Questions

Q1. What is included in the Free version vs. RoastBite Pro?

The Free plan includes up to 5 meal logs and roasts per day, full access to Savage Chef and Hype Bestie personas, Today's Fuel Score & Treat-vs-Fuel breakdown, and 9:16 Share Roast Cards. RoastBite Pro unlocks unlimited daily food logs, all 4 personas (adding Strict Mom and Zen Monk), optional watermark-free Share Cards, and complete 7-day weekly insights.

Q2. Does RoastBite require internet or an AI account?

No. RoastBite's 96-keyword natural-language food parser and 192 original persona roasts run 100% offline on your iPhone or iPad—so it never lags, never hits server outages, and never uploads your food diary anywhere.

Q3. How do I share a 9:16 Roast Card to TikTok, Instagram Stories, or X?

Tap "Share Roast Card" on any roast bubble in Log & Roast (or the Share button in Today). RoastBite renders a crisp 1080x1920 9:16 PNG card directly through the native iOS Share Sheet with zero photo-library permissions required.

Q4. How do I restore my Pro purchase on a new device?

Sign in with the same Apple ID you used for your purchase, open Settings in RoastBite, and tap "Restore Purchases." Your active subscription or Lifetime unlock will reactivate immediately.
